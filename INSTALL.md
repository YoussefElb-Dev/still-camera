# Install Still Camera

## Android

Copy `Still-Camera.apk` to your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow the app you opened it with to install unknown apps, then choose Install. Open Still Camera and allow camera access. Allow photo access when you want to save captures to your phone's gallery.

This personal build supports Android 7.0 and newer. Keep the existing installation when updating with another APK signed with the same key; uninstalling also deletes photos stored only inside the app.

## iPhone

`Still-Camera-unsigned.ipa` must be signed with your Apple account before your iPhone can install it. You can do this from Windows or macOS with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/index.html), or use [AltStore Classic](https://faq.altstore.io/altstore-classic/your-altstore).

For Sideloadly, connect your iPhone to the computer, trust the computer when prompted, select the IPA, choose your device, and sign in with your Apple account inside Sideloadly. Follow its prompts to install. If iOS asks for Developer Mode or trust for your developer profile, follow the on-screen instructions before opening Still Camera. Never put your Apple password in this repository or send it in chat.

Sideloadly supports free Apple accounts. Apps signed with a free account expire after seven days and need refreshing; its automatic refresh can handle this while your device and computer are available. See the [official Sideloadly FAQ](https://sideloadly.io/faq.html). The app itself has no subscription, ads, paid effects, or account requirement.

This build targets iOS 15 and newer. Camera hardware behavior still needs checking on a physical iPhone.

## Take a photo

Tap the small camera illustration beside the shutter to choose a camera look. Tap the large white button to capture. The thumbnail beside it opens your saved photos. The import button lets you apply a look to an existing photo.

Open Camera settings using the sliders button. **High resolution** is on by default and keeps the resolution supplied by your camera. Switch it off for a 1600-pixel retro export. **Keep original too** preserves an untouched copy, and **Save to Photos** adds your processed captures to the system photo library.

All ten camera looks and effects are available without payment.
