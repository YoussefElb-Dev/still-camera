# Privacy

Still Camera processes images on your device. It does not create an account, collect usage analytics, serve advertising, upload your photo library, or send photos to a server. A sample image and all effects are bundled in the app, so normal photography works offline.

Camera permission enables the live viewfinder and capture. Microphone recording is disabled. Photo-library permission is used to import photos or save your captures to Photos. Android 10 and older may request storage permission for that save operation. The app does not request location access.

Captures are saved in the app’s private Still folder and indexed locally. If Save to Photos is enabled, an additional copy is placed in the system photo library. Deleting a capture in Still removes its app-private copy and matching optional original; it does not remove copies already exported to Photos. Uninstalling the app removes its private photos. Export important photos first.

Share opens your operating system’s share sheet. The destination you choose receives that selected photo under its own privacy policy. The app does not share anything automatically.
